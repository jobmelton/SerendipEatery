# Witness and steward plan

## Independent witnesses (minimum two)

Guinness requires at least two witnesses who are not employees of the organiser and have no financial interest in the result.

Each witness will:

1. Be named in the application before registration opens  
2. Receive read-only credentials to the live roster, live bracket, and hash chain  
3. Attend (in person or by video) the freeze and bracket-generation ceremony  
4. Watch the final  
5. Sign the official witness statement using the Guinness template  
6. Keep their own notes of the official count at freeze, after round 1, and at close  

Invite, in writing, people who already have a public reputation for neutrality: a local journalist, a university faculty member, an accountant, or an attorney. Do not use friends who will play.

## Digital stewards

In-person mass-participation records often require one steward per 50 people. Ask Guinness whether a digital event needs that ratio.

Until they answer, operate as if the answer is yes:

- A steward dashboard lists a slice of 50 official participants  
- The steward can flag a suspicious account (duplicate name + similar phone, burst registrations, impossible lock timing)  
- Flags go to the organiser and both witnesses  
- Stewards cannot change match outcomes  

At 16,384 official participants that is 328 stewards. Recruit from:

- restaurant partners (they benefit from the unlock)  
- volunteer community organisers  
- a paid steward pool if volunteers fall short  

If Guinness waives the 1:50 ratio for a digital event, keep a smaller audit team (suggested: 20) and document the waiver.

## Timekeepers

The attempt is not a timed endurance record. Deadlines are server timestamps in UTC. Two witnesses confirm that the server clock is NTP-synced at freeze. No separate timekeeper booth is planned unless Guinness requires one.

## Ceremony run-of-show (freeze day)

1. Witnesses join the call. Wall clock shown.  
2. Organiser reads the rules version string.  
3. System shows verified-registration count.  
4. Organiser hits freeze. Unverified rows drop. New count is read aloud.  
5. System publishes the freeze seed and roster hash.  
6. Bracket is generated. Bracket hash is published.  
7. Round 1 SMS goes out.  
8. Witnesses sign a short contemporaneous note: counts, hashes, time.

## Public transparency

- Live verified-registration counter during the open window  
- Live official-count and live bracket after freeze  
- Hash explorer for the event log  
- No public access to phone numbers or emails
